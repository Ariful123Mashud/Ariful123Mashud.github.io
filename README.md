# 💫 About Me:
Ariful Mashud, is a researcher and educator. He holds a Ph.D. in Control Systems and has over 3 years of industrial experience in Automotive Research and Development, specializing in advanced steering systems, vehicle dynamics, and intelligent control technologies. His research focuses on real-time controller design, autonomous vehicle systems, and the application of machine learning and deep learning to intelligent control and mobility systems.<br>
